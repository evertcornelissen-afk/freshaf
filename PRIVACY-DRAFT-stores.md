# DRAFT — FreshAF Privacy Policy update for the app stores

**Status: APPROVED by Evert and PUBLISHED 7 Nov 2026** at https://freshaf.io/privacy
(`public/privacy.html`, "Last updated: 7 November 2026"). This file is kept as the record.

**Why now:** both stores check that the privacy policy matches the privacy answers given in
the store (Google Play Data safety, Apple App Privacy). The live policy (12 July 2026) still
names PayFast, and does not mention WhatsApp, phone notifications, location, photos or the
providers that process data for us. A mismatch is a common rejection reason.

---

## 1. What we collect

- **Account details:** name, email, mobile number, preferred address, password (stored only as
  a salted hash; nobody at FreshAF can see it).
- **Order details:** the service address and map pin you choose, package, notes, order history,
  ratings you give.
- **Location:** your precise location only when you tap "Use my location" to drop the pin
  (customers), or to set your work area (pros). You can always type an address instead.
- **Photos:** pros take a photo when laundry is handed over, as proof of delivery. It is visible
  only to the customer, the pro and FreshAF.
- **Pros only:** business name, South African ID number, bank details, vehicle and equipment
  details, documents you upload. ID numbers and bank details are encrypted when stored.
- **Phone notifications:** if you allow notifications, a notification token for your phone
  (from Google Firebase Cloud Messaging on Android, Apple Push Notification service on iPhone),
  used only to send you booking and job alerts. Switch them off any time in the app's Settings.
- **Payments:** card payments are made on our payment provider's (Stitch) secure page. FreshAF
  never sees or stores your card number.

We do not use advertising IDs, we show no ads, and we do not track you across other apps or
websites.

## 2. How we use it

- To match your booking with a nearby pro, work out travel fees, and run the booking.
- To send booking updates and job alerts by email, phone notification and WhatsApp.
- To take payments, give rewards, refund when needed, and provide support.
- To vet and approve pros and keep the platform safe.

We do not sell personal information.

## 3. Who receives it

- **The other side of your booking:** when a pro takes your booking they see your name, service
  address, contact number and notes; you see the pro's business name, first name, rating and
  contact number. Only for the booking.
- **Service providers who process data for us,** under contract and only for these purposes:
  hosting (Render, Cloudflare), card payments (Stitch), email (GoDaddy), WhatsApp messages to
  pros and the FreshAF team (Meta, WhatsApp Business Platform), phone notifications (Google
  Firebase Cloud Messaging, Apple Push Notification service), and map tiles (OpenStreetMap,
  which receives the map area you view).
- Some of these providers store data outside South Africa. We use them only with safeguards
  that POPIA (section 72) requires.

## 4. Keeping and deleting it

Delete your account in the app (Account → Delete account) or at freshaf.io/delete-account.
Deletion removes your identity immediately; booking and payment records are kept in anonymised
form for as long as tax and accounting law requires. Handover photos are kept with the order.

## 5. Your rights

Under POPIA you may ask to see, correct or delete your personal information, object to its
use, and complain to the Information Regulator. Contact us first at admin@freshaf.io; we reply
within 30 days.

## 6. Security

Everything is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Passwords are hashed; ID numbers and bank details
are encrypted at rest; access to production data is restricted. In the app, your sign-in is
kept in the phone's encrypted storage. No system is perfectly secure: report a suspected breach
to admin@freshaf.io.

## 7. Children

FreshAF is for people aged 18 and over.
