## How to read this list

| Tier | Who they are | Why they matter |
|---|---|---|
| **A — Recruit** | Fixed-site car washes and laundromats with idle capacity, plus small independent operators | These want more jobs. They are your supply. Lead with "your phone rings, you keep 85%". |
| **B — Partner or compete** | Established mobile brands that already have their own booking systems | Either a channel partner (they take overflow) or a direct competitor. Study their pricing and coverage. |

**Recruitment rule:** Tier A first. A laundromat with three idle machines at 10am is worth more to you
than a slick mobile brand that already owns its demand.

## What this tells us about the market

1. **The Northern Suburbs are already served — but fragmented.** No single brand owns both services.
   WashDoc owns mobile car wash; Luxury Laundry owns premium laundry collection. Nobody offers both
   in one app with one login. That gap is exactly FreshAF's position.
2. **Pricing check.** Luxury Laundry charges R55/kg wash & fold. A typical 8 kg load = R440 — nearly
   double our small-load price. We are cheap on small loads and expensive on large ones. Worth
   reviewing whether our load sizes are priced correctly for this market.
3. **Callout benchmark.** WashDoc charges R100 beyond its free zone, waived over R750. Our free-5km
   then R10/km model is in the same territory and defensible.
4. **The best recruits are the fixed sites.** Thunder Brothers (5 branches in our exact area),
   24 Hour Laundry (4 branches, 24-hour capacity) and Laundry Dry Cleaning 4U (8 sites) each represent
   multiple suburbs from a single conversation.

## Recommended call order

1. **Soapy Suds** — small, local, already collects. Fastest yes.
2. **Thunder Brothers** — one deal, five suburbs of car wash capacity.
3. **24 Hour Laundry** — overnight idle capacity is free money for them.
4. **Laundry Dry Cleaning 4U** — 8 sites, already runs pickups.
5. **Eco Mobile Valet / Traffik / The Bay Wash** — independents already mobile.

Leave WashDoc and Luxury Laundry until you have your own supply — approaching a direct competitor
from a position of weakness invites them to copy the model rather than join it.
