## 1. What this agreement is

1.1 FreshAF sells mobile car wash and laundry services to its own customers. FreshAF contracts
with the customer, prices the job, invoices the customer and collects payment in its own name.

1.2 **FreshAF buys the performance of those jobs from the Supplier.** The Supplier is an
independent business supplying services to FreshAF. The Supplier has no contract with, and no
claim against, the customer.

1.3 This agreement governs every job FreshAF buys from the Supplier. It does not oblige FreshAF to
offer any job, and it does not oblige the Supplier to accept any job.

## 2. The Supplier is an independent contractor

2.1 The Supplier is **not an employee**, agent, partner or joint venturer of FreshAF. Nothing in
this agreement creates an employment relationship, and the Supplier is not entitled to any
employment benefit.

2.2 The Supplier:

- (a) decides when and whether to make themselves available for work;
- (b) accepts or declines each job offered, at their sole discretion and without penalty;
- (c) **sets their own prices** (clause 4);
- (d) sets their own service radius;
- (e) decides how the work is performed, within the standards in clause 6;
- (f) supplies their own equipment, materials, consumables, water, power and transport;
- (g) engages and pays their own helpers or employees, if any, and remains responsible for them;
- (h) **is free to supply the same or similar services to any other person, including to FreshAF's
  competitors and to their own customers.** FreshAF claims no exclusivity.

2.3 FreshAF does not set the Supplier's hours, does not require the Supplier to wear FreshAF
branding, and does not supervise how the work is done.

2.4 The Supplier is responsible for their own income tax, provisional tax, VAT (if registered),
UIF, workers' compensation in respect of their own staff, and all licences and permits their
business requires. **FreshAF deducts no employees' tax and makes no statutory deductions.**

2.5 The Supplier must inform FreshAF immediately if they register for VAT or if their VAT status
changes.

## 3. How jobs are offered and accepted

3.1 FreshAF publishes customer requests through the FreshAF Pro application. The Supplier may
respond with a price and their availability.

3.2 A price submitted by the Supplier is **an offer to FreshAF**, open for the period stated in the
application. If FreshAF accepts it, a binding engagement arises for that job at that price and at
the stated availability.

3.3 Where a job is scheduled for a specific date and time, submitting a price is a commitment to
that slot as well as to the price.

3.4 Failing to attend an accepted job, or attending materially late, is a breach of that
engagement. Repeated failures are a ground for suspension under clause 9.

## 4. Price and payment

4.1 **The Supplier sets the price at which FreshAF buys each job.** The Supplier may publish a
price list in the application, which responds to matching requests automatically, or price each
job individually. Published prices may be changed at any time and apply to requests received after
the change.

4.2 FreshAF pays the Supplier **the price the Supplier quoted, in full**, together with any travel
or callout amount shown with that job. **No commission, platform fee or other deduction is taken
from the Supplier's price.**

4.3 FreshAF prices the job to the customer at its own discretion, by adding its own margin.
**That margin is FreshAF's revenue, is not deducted from the Supplier, and does not affect what the
Supplier is paid.** The Supplier has no interest in, and no claim to, the price FreshAF charges
the customer.

4.4 The Supplier must invoice FreshAF for completed jobs, or may agree in writing that FreshAF
issues a self-billed statement in place of the Supplier's invoice. FreshAF pays
**[weekly / within X days]** of an accepted invoice or statement into the bank account the
Supplier nominates. The Supplier must keep those banking details accurate and current.

4.5 **Cash jobs.** Where a customer pays cash, the Supplier collects the full amount **as
FreshAF's collection agent**. That money belongs to FreshAF. The Supplier may retain their own
price from it and must account to FreshAF for the balance. The running balance is shown in the
Supplier's earnings screen, is payable on demand, and **FreshAF may set it off** against any
amount FreshAF owes the Supplier.

4.6 FreshAF may withhold payment for a job that was not performed, was not completed, or is the
subject of a customer claim, until the claim is resolved.

## 5. Onboarding, vetting and records

5.1 Approval to supply is at FreshAF's discretion and may be refused, suspended or withdrawn,
including on the outcome of identity, criminal or document checks.

5.2 The Supplier warrants that every document and statement supplied at onboarding is true, and
must tell FreshAF within **[7]** days if any of it changes.

5.3 The Supplier consents to FreshAF processing their personal information for vetting, dispatch,
payment, tax and dispute purposes, in accordance with POPIA and FreshAF's Privacy Policy.

## 6. Standards

6.1 The Supplier must perform each job with the care and skill reasonably expected of a competent
professional in their trade, using safe and serviceable equipment, and in compliance with all
applicable law and any site rules at the customer's address.

6.2 The Supplier must not subcontract a job to anyone else without FreshAF's prior written
consent. The Supplier remains responsible for any helper they use.

6.3 The Supplier must report any damage, loss, injury or incident to FreshAF **immediately**, and
must not negotiate or settle a claim directly with a customer.

## 7. Customers belong to FreshAF

7.1 Customers introduced through FreshAF are FreshAF's customers. The Supplier must not solicit or
accept work directly from a FreshAF customer in respect of a job offered through FreshAF, and must
not use customer contact details for any purpose other than performing that job.

7.2 Clause 7.1 does not prevent the Supplier from serving their own customers, or from being
approached independently by a member of the public. **It is not a restraint of trade.**

## 8. Liability and indemnity

8.1 FreshAF is the supplier of the service to the customer and is accountable to the customer for
it.

8.2 **The Supplier indemnifies FreshAF** against all loss, damage, cost, refund, penalty and claim
arising from the Supplier's acts or omissions, including defective or incomplete work, damage to a
customer's property or vehicle, loss of a customer's items, injury caused by the Supplier or their
helpers, use of unsafe equipment, and failure to attend an accepted job.

8.3 The Supplier must maintain **[public liability insurance of not less than R\_\_\_\_\_\_]** in
respect of the services, and must produce proof on request.

> 💬 **Note for Nikki.** An indemnity from a sole trader with no assets is close to worthless in
> practice. Either FreshAF carries its own public liability cover for subcontracted work, or
> clause 8.3 is enforced at onboarding with real proof. Recommend the former, because it is
> within FreshAF's control and does not block supply.

8.4 Neither party is liable to the other for indirect or consequential loss.

## 9. Suspension and termination

9.1 Either party may terminate this agreement on **[7]** days' written notice, without reason.

9.2 FreshAF may suspend or terminate immediately on a material breach, a failure of a vetting
check, conduct that endangers a customer or FreshAF's reputation, or repeated no-shows or quality
complaints.

9.3 Termination does not affect accrued payment obligations, clause 4.5 (cash balances owed),
clause 7, or clause 8.

## 10. General

10.1 This agreement, together with the FreshAF Terms & Conditions, is the whole agreement between
the parties. **Where this agreement and the Terms & Conditions differ, this agreement prevails.**

10.2 FreshAF may vary this agreement on **[14]** days' written notice. Continuing to supply after
that date constitutes acceptance.

10.3 The Supplier may not cede or assign this agreement.

10.4 South African law governs this agreement, and the parties submit to the jurisdiction of the
Magistrate's Court.

10.5 Notices to FreshAF go to `admin@freshaf.io`; notices to the Supplier go to the email and
mobile number on their FreshAF Pro account.
