Delete your FreshAF account

You can delete your FreshAF account yourself, at any time, without asking us and without giving a reason. This page explains how, and exactly what is removed.

Delete it yourself, in the app

This is the fastest way and it takes effect immediately.

  1. Open FreshAF and sign in.
  2. Go to Account.
  3. Tap Delete account, enter your password to confirm, and tap Delete my account.

Open my account

If you cannot sign in

Email admin@freshaf.io from the address on the account, with the subject Account deletion request. We will verify that the request comes from the account holder and delete the account within 7 days. We will not ask you to telephone us, and we will not try to talk you out of it.

What is deleted

Deleting is permanent. It is not a deactivation and it cannot be undone — signing up again later creates a brand-new account with no history.

Deleted immediately Your name, email address, telephone number, password, saved home address and map pin, saved vehicles, reward points, and your push-notification subscriptions. If you are a Pro: your business name, ID number, vehicle registration, service area, equipment notes and banking details, and your profile is taken offline so you receive no further jobs.
Kept, without your identity Completed orders are kept as anonymised financial records — the service, the price, the date and the VAT. They are no longer linked to a person. South African tax and company law require us to keep transaction records for five years, and we are not permitted to delete them on request.
Not kept at all Card numbers. FreshAF never stores them — they are entered on our payment provider's own pages and never reach us.

Before you delete

  • Finish or cancel any order that is still running. Deleting does not cancel a job that a Pro is already on their way to.
  • Reward points are forfeited. They have no cash value and cannot be transferred or paid out.
  • Pros: money we already owe you for completed jobs is still paid, and any cash balance you owe FreshAF is still payable. Deleting your account does not settle either one. Settle up with us first so nothing is left hanging.

Your other rights

Under the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 you may also ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you, or ask us to correct it, without deleting your account. Email admin@freshaf.io. See our Privacy Policy for the full detail, and our contact page for our company details.
