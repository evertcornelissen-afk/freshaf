What we do

FreshAF supplies mobile car washing and laundry collection and delivery to customers in South Africa, booked online and delivered at the address the customer chooses.

FreshAF is the supplier of the service and the merchant of record. The customer's contract is with FRESHAF (PTY) LTD, we invoice and take payment in our own name, and we are accountable for the work. The work is carried out on our behalf by independent, vetted service professionals whom we engage and pay as our subcontractors. Customers choose which of the available professionals attends their job.

Prices and payment

  • All prices are quoted and charged in South African Rand (ZAR).
  • Catalogue prices shown before you request prices are indicative and marked “from”.
  • Customers pay FreshAF a single price per job, shown in full before they confirm. Travel to the address, and the size of the vehicle or load, are already included. No service fee, booking fee, commission or surcharge is added, and the customer is never charged more than the amount displayed at confirmation.
  • Prices include VAT where VAT is applicable.
  • Payment is by card, taken by FreshAF through our payment provider, or cash, collected on completion by the professional on FreshAF's behalf. Receipts and tax invoices are issued by FreshAF.
  • Full pricing terms: Terms & Conditions, section 3.

Delivery and performance

  • Services are performed at the address you pin when booking, inside the attending professional's chosen travel radius.
  • You may book for as soon as possible, or schedule a specific date and time up to 30 days ahead. Prices are quoted against that time, and the attending professional confirms the slot.
  • Laundry turnaround times are shown per package at booking.

Cancellations and refunds

  • Cancel free of charge while we are finding a professional, or before they start travelling to you. Once work has started an order cannot be cancelled.
  • Card payments for cancelled orders are refunded in full by FreshAF to the original payment method. Rewards used are returned to your balance.
  • Full terms: Terms & Conditions, section 6.

Complaints and disputes

Email admin@freshaf.io with your order number. We aim to respond within one working day. Damage or loss claims have their own reporting deadlines — 24 hours for car wash, 48 hours for laundry — set out in Terms & Conditions, sections 8 and 9.

Security and personal information

The site is served over HTTPS. Card details are entered on our payment provider's secure pages and are never stored by FreshAF. We process personal information in accordance with the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013. See our Privacy Policy.
