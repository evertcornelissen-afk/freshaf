What we do

FreshAF is an online marketplace connecting customers with independent, vetted service professionals for mobile car washing and laundry collection and delivery in South Africa. The service is performed by the professional you select; FreshAF provides the booking, quoting, dispatch, tracking and payment technology.

Prices and payment

  • All prices are quoted and charged in South African Rand (ZAR).
  • Each professional sets their own price. Catalogue prices shown before you request quotes are indicative and marked “from”.
  • Your total — the professional's price, any vehicle or load adjustment, the callout fee and the FreshAF service fee — is shown in full before you confirm. You are never charged more than the amount displayed at confirmation.
  • Payment is by card, processed by our payment provider, or cash to the professional on completion.
  • Full pricing terms: Terms & Conditions, section 3.

Delivery and performance

  • Services are performed at the address you pin when booking, inside the professional's chosen travel radius.
  • You may book for as soon as possible, or schedule a specific date and time up to 30 days ahead. The professional quotes against that time and confirms whether they can make it.
  • Laundry turnaround times are shown per package at booking.

Cancellations and refunds

  • Cancel free of charge while we are finding a professional, or before they start travelling to you. Once work has started an order cannot be cancelled.
  • Card payments for cancelled orders are refunded in full, including the service fee, to the original payment method. Rewards used are returned to your balance.
  • Full terms: Terms & Conditions, section 6.

Complaints and disputes

Email support@freshaf.co.za with your order number. We aim to respond within one working day. Damage or loss claims have their own reporting deadlines — 24 hours for car wash, 48 hours for laundry — set out in Terms & Conditions, sections 8 and 9.

Security and personal information

The site is served over HTTPS. Card details are entered on our payment provider's secure pages and are never stored by FreshAF. We process personal information in accordance with the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013. See our Privacy Policy.
