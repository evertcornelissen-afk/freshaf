## Section 8 (Laundry-specific terms) — add these four points

- **Return to a different address.** You can ask for your laundry to be brought back to an
  address other than the one it was collected from. You must give that address, any building
  or access details, and the name of the person or desk (for example a reception) who will
  receive it. The return trip is included in the one price you are shown before you confirm.

- **Return time.** The earliest return time depends on the service: 24 hours after collection
  for Wash & Fold, 48 hours for the other laundry services. Returns are made between 07:00 and
  18:00, and you may choose a time up to 7 days after the earliest. We aim to arrive within two
  hours of the time you choose; it is a target, not a guaranteed delivery time.

- **Handover.** Delivery is complete when the laundry is handed to you or to the receiver you
  named. The Pro records the name of the person who received it and takes a photo at the
  handover, and we send you that record. The photo is used only as proof of delivery, is
  visible only to you, the Pro and FreshAF, and is kept with your order. From the handover the
  laundry is your responsibility, and the 48-hour period for reporting damage or loss (above)
  starts then.

- **If no one can receive it.** If nobody is available to receive the laundry at the return
  address during the window you chose, the Pro will try to contact you and will keep your
  laundry safe. We will arrange a second delivery attempt with you, and that second attempt is
  charged at the return-trip rate shown in the app. Until it is handed over, your laundry
  remains our responsibility.

## Section 11 (Terms for Pros) — add this point

- **Laundry handovers.** When you deliver laundry you must, in FreshAF Pro, photograph the
  items being handed over and record the name of the person who received them. Hand laundry
  only to the receiver the customer named, or to the customer. A laundry job is not complete
  until the handover is recorded.

## Privacy policy — matching change also needed

The privacy policy lists photos as "Job condition photos". Handover photos are a new purpose
and can show the person receiving the laundry. Add: *"Handover photos: when a Pro delivers
laundry they photograph the handover as proof of delivery. The photo is visible to you, the
Pro and FreshAF only, and is kept with the order record."* (While editing, the privacy policy
still names PayFast as the card processor; it is Stitch.)
